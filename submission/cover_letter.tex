    \item \textbf{Causal Attribution of Algorithmic Gain (Major 1, 4):}
    We have corrected the causal attribution of the matched ablation gain. The factor-by-factor comparison in Table~6 clearly demonstrates that essentially the entire algorithmic gain ($\Delta r = +0.054 \pm 0.006$ over white-noise GLS; $+0.0568 \pm 0.0046$ in Row~D vs.\ Row~A, $p_t = 5.9\times10^{-7}$) arises from per-dwell-slot nuisance profiling, not from the Ornstein--Uhlenbeck (OU) temporal covariance.     We withdrew the claim that residual spin misalignments can be refined in flight by minimizing photometric residuals.
